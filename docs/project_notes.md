# Project Notes

## Project Focus

This project focuses on SQL data analysis using a Gold layer dataset with customer, product, and sales information.

The main goal was to transform business data into useful analytical outputs, including KPIs, customer segmentation, product performance analysis, and reporting views that could support dashboards or business decision-making.

## Business Questions Covered

This project helps answer questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most to sales?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which product categories represent the largest share of revenue?
- How can customers be segmented based on spending and purchase history?
- Which products perform better or worse compared to previous years?
- What KPIs can be used to summarize customer and product performance?

## Main KPIs Analyzed

The project includes several business KPIs commonly used in data analytics:

### Sales KPIs

- Total sales
- Total quantity sold
- Average selling price
- Average order revenue
- Average monthly revenue

### Customer KPIs

- Total customers
- Total orders
- Recency
- Average order value
- Average monthly spend
- Customer lifespan
- Customer segment

### Product KPIs

- Total product revenue
- Total orders by product
- Total customers by product
- Product recency
- Product segment
- Year-over-year sales change

## Final Reporting Views

The project creates two final reporting views:

- `gold.report_customers`: summarizes customer behavior, spending, order activity, recency, age groups, and customer segments.
- `gold.report_products`: summarizes product performance, revenue, quantity sold, customer reach, recency, and product segments.

These views are designed to be used as a base for dashboards, business reports, or further analysis.

## Key Takeaway

This project helped me practice how SQL can be used to move from raw data exploration to business-focused reporting.

The most valuable part was learning how to organize analytical queries into a clear workflow and create final views that summarize the most important KPIs for customers and products.
